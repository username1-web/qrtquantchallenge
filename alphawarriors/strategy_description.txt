Strategy Description – AlphaWarriors

Core Idea
A robust, high-performance absolute-return systematic strategy combining cross-sectional single-factor mean reversion and price-volume trend-following. By completely removing reliance on black-box non-linear Machine Learning modules, the strategy is mathematically grounded, exceptionally fast to evaluate, devoid of chronic over-fitting, and boasts significant out-of-sample resilience.

Signals
Our research identified that 1-day lagged signals often predict strong short-term price movements across the daily tradable universe.
We use two classes of signals:
- Mean Reversion: 6 specific technical/oscillator factors (e.g. accumulation_distribution_index, ichimoku, trend_5_20, trend_1_3, chaikin_money_flow, average_true_range) heavily correlated with near-term reversals. These are ranked cross-sectionally descending.
- Volume & Trend: 3 positive predictive volume/flow factors (volume, on_balance_volume, ease_of_movement) showing strong cross-sectional persistence. These are ranked cross-sectionally ascending.

All daily signals are explicitly cross-sectionally ranked using `method='first'` and demeaned to represent continuous, symmetrical score equivalents devoid of any unbounded feature outliers.

Portfolio Formation
1. Negative IC factors (Mean Reversion) are aggregated equal-weight.
2. Positive IC factors (Volume) are aggressively overlaid with a specific optimized weighting structure (0.7).
3. The raw aggregate score enforces universe masking constraints daily.
4. Apply an Exponential Weighted Moving Average (EWMA) with a span of 5 days across the signal. This crucially controls our portfolio turnover limit—drastically cutting operational trading costs while smoothly bridging any single-day noisy reversals.
5. Re-demean the signals explicitly over solely the tradable space.
6. The resulting vector is accurately normalized absolutely such that the daily final gross constraint is tightly held to exactly 1.0 (with 0.5 Long and 0.5 Short respectively).

Risk Controls
- Eliminating all complex multi-feature tree combinations isolates factor returns solely to the proven core anomalies. 
- Using percentage ranks effectively neutralizes long-tailed technical indicator values daily.
- Setting an EWMA limits turnover to safe and highly cost-efficient turnover bounds out-of-sample. Maximum single-asset stock weight bounds trivially hold implicitly due to wide diverse alpha portfolios lacking non-linear scaling explosions.
